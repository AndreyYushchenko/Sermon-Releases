Features

Everything Sunday needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Any song in seconds.

By number, title or a line of the lyrics. Your songbooks and chords, with verses split into slides by themselves.

Projector and stream at once.

The same slide on the projector, a second screen and OBS.

Run it from your phone.

Step through slides, show songs and message the operator from an iPhone or iPad.

Type “Psalm 23”. It’s on screen.

Any translation you load, with a second one under the verse if you like.

Countdowns, clocks and messages.

Count down to the service, time a break or put a clock on the whole screen. This is how the room sees them.

Livestream

Slides in OBS.
No capture card.

Sermon gives OBS a page with the current slide. Add it once and it follows the projector from then on.

  1. Copy the address in Sermon
  2. Add a Browser source in OBS and paste it
  3. Done. When nothing is on screen, the camera shows through
