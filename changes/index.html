What’s new

Every version in short: how it was and how it is now.

Sermon 0.6.0 beta

New25.11.2026

  • Songbooks

    BeforeA song already in another songbook was left out

    NowEvery songbook arrives complete, with all of its songs

  • Song import

    BeforeOnly SoftProjector songbooks, ChordPro and plain text

    NowAlso OpenLyrics, OpenSong, ProPresenter, SongSelect and SongBeamer

  • New look

    BeforeOn small laptops the panels squeezed each other

    NowA light design, and on small screens the sidebar folds to icons

  • Slide marks

    BeforeOnly the slide on screen was marked

    NowBlue: on screen, orange: shown before it, green: where you left a song

  • French

    BeforeSermon had no French

    NowSermon, the website and the bot now speak French too

  • Support

    BeforeYou could only write by email

    NowHelp has a Telegram button, and you can support the project from the sidebar

Sermon 0.5.1 beta

19.11.2026

  • Presentations on the Mac

    BeforeThe presentation module didn’t download on the Mac

    NowIt downloads and starts, and a failure shows the real reason

  • The selection

    BeforeStayed put when slides changed by arrows or a clicker

    NowFollows the slide on screen, which gets a green frame, as in songs

  • Hide

    BeforeAfter “Hide”, arrows or a clicker brought the slide back

    NowThe screen stays hidden until you show a slide yourself

  • Song slides

    BeforeThe number took a band across the top, lines broke with one word left

    NowThe number sits in its corner, long lines split into even halves

Sermon 0.5.0 beta

14.11.2026

  • Pasted songs

    BeforeWith only the chorus marked, everything after it landed on one slide

    NowEach paragraph becomes a verse, with the chorus after every one

  • Text size

    BeforeThe size jumped from verse to verse

    NowThe same size on every slide of a song

  • Numbered verses

    Before“1.”, “2.” at the start of a stanza weren’t recognised

    Now“1.”, “2.” mark verses and “0.” marks the chorus

  • Text in OBS

    BeforeAfter a long pause the words sometimes went missing on the stream

    NowThe words appear every time

  • Preview

    BeforeBroke lines and sized text differently from the projector

    NowExactly as on the screen

  • Settings

    BeforeWere scattered around and repeated

    NowEverything in one place, your settings kept
